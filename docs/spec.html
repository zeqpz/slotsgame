Platform reality — two brief items adapted

Stake Engine pre-simulates every outcome into weighted “books”; the RGS draws one per bet and no state survives between rounds. So the Full Wall progressive becomes an in-round climax: paint all 5 reels during free spins → mural completes → 5,000× wincap. Base-game drips persist within their spin only; full drip persistence lives in the bonus, which is one round internally. A cosmetic cross-session “career wall” can still live client-side.

02 Paint Drips

  • A landed drip expands vertically and paints its whole reel wild (around scatters/characters).
  • Color bonus: each winning line pays 1 + painted-reels-crossed × paint-level. Paint level: +1× base and standard bonus · +2× at Extreme start · Tag Meter upgrades to +3×.
  • Base game: drips live on reels 2–4 of the strips; the paint lasts that spin.
  • Free spins: painted reels are sticky for the entire bonus; new drips arrive via the per-spin landing_drips tables (free-spin strips carry no W — drip rate is a pure tuning dial).

03 Characters

Characters pay as premiums and multiply the whole spin when it wins: values sum within a type (stacks) and multiply across types — the brief’s 3× + 5× = 15× rule — capped at 128×. Values are drawn per landing: C1 = 2×/3×, C2 = 5×, C3 = 8×/10× (free spins skew the draws higher).

04 Bonuses, Tag Meter, Full Wall

Standard Bonus

3/4/5 Crew Leaders → 8/10/12 free spins. Sticky drips, +1× paint, Tag Meter target 4.

Extreme Bonus

Direct: 2 Phantoms → 10 spins. Upgrade: standard trigger + a Phantom on the same spin. Painted reels from the trigger spin carry over, paint starts at +2×, richer character draws, Tag Meter target 3.

Tag Meter

Every winning free spin adds a tag. Full meter → +2 free spins (max +6 per bonus) and a paint-level upgrade, then it resets. No scatter retriggers — the meter is the extension mechanic, which keeps rounds bounded.

Full Wall

4 / 5 sections — one reel from the mural

Each painted reel fills a mural section. All five during free spins = Full Wall: the round pays up to the 5,000× cap and the bonus ends on the finished piece. Raw sim frequency ~0.25% of standard and ~7% of extreme bonuses; live frequency is shaped further down by optimizer weights.

06 Math architecture

  • Sims split into forced criteria per mode — wincap, extremegame, freegame, basegame, 0 — with custom board forcing that plants exact Crew Leader and Phantom counts on one board.
  • Five deterministic strips (gen_reels.py): BR0 base · BRE base-with-Phantoms · FR0 / FRE free spins · FRWCAP drip-rich wincap strip. Live extreme frequency comes from optimizer weights, not strip odds.
  • Wincap books converge organically: the hot strip completes the wall, and the wall pays exactly the cap.
  • analyze_books.py prints per-criteria payout distributions, wall %, painted-section and character-multiplier histograms for every tuning pass.
  • Calibration lesson: sticky full-reel wilds × total-spin multipliers compound violently — the first cut walled ~100% of bonuses. Final drip EV ≈ 0.10–0.13 per spin with character density halved.
